A flexible digest type for use cases not covered by the standard digest types. Configurable scope, scheduling, and output for one-off or unusual reporting needs.

**The Custom Digest is the flexible catch-all type.** When the Weekly Coaching Plan, Executive / Leadership Digest, and Daily IC Briefing don't fit the job you need a digest for, the Custom Digest gives you the configurability to build something that does.

If you find yourself describing what you need with phrases like _"a weekly review of strategic accounts"_ or _"a daily release recap"_ or _"a monthly summary of one specific segment"_ — that's a Custom Digest.

## Why this digest[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#why-this-digest)

The standard digest types are intentionally tuned for specific roles and jobs. The Coaching Plan is built around 1:1 prep. The Executive Digest is built around weekly leadership review. The Daily IC Briefing is built around start-of-day account triage. Each is the right tool for its job, and that specificity is what makes them useful.

But not every reporting need fits one of those three jobs. You might want:

- A weekly review focused on one specific customer segment, scoped narrower than an Executive Digest but broader than a single Coaching Plan would allow
    
- A daily summary of a specific high-priority initiative or rollout
    
- A monthly digest of cross-functional collaboration patterns
    
- A weekly review of accounts at a specific lifecycle stage (e.g., onboarding accounts past day 90)
    
- A digest for a non-CS audience — finance, product, exec ops — who need a slice of the data the standard types don't deliver
    

The Custom Digest is for those.

## What it contains[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#what-it-contains)

A Custom Digest is more open-ended than the standard types. The exact content depends on how you configure it, but the building blocks are:

- **The scope you define** — accounts, teams, [Squads](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/squads-and-segments), [Segments](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/squads-and-segments), customer lifecycle stage, or any combination
    
- **The** [**KPIs**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/kpis) **you select** — pick the metrics relevant to the digest's purpose
    
- **The cadence and lookback you choose** — daily, weekly, monthly, with whatever lookback window matches the cadence
    
- **AI-generated narrative** based on the selected scope and KPIs, plus the standard signals Lumopath surfaces
    

The output looks similar to the other digest types — a structured email with metric summaries, narrative analysis, and links back to the relevant dashboards — but the content is shaped by your configuration rather than a role-specific template.

## Who it's for[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#who-its-for)

Anyone with a reporting need that doesn't fit the other three types. Custom Digests are most often used by:

- **Operations and RevOps leads** building cross-functional reports
    
- **Specialist teams** (onboarding, renewals, churn prevention) tracking metrics specific to their function
    
- **Project leads** running a recurring report on a specific initiative or rollout
    
- **Managers** with an unusual reporting cadence (e.g., a daily morning summary on top of their weekly Coaching Plan)
    
- **Cross-functional consumers** (Sales, Product, Finance) who want a slice of CS or AM data
    

## Setup[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#setup)

The general digest creation flow is covered in [Setting Up a Digest](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/setting-up-a-digest). The Custom Digest's configuration is more open-ended than the other types.

### Digest Name[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#digest-name)

Custom Digests benefit more than other types from clear, specific names — because you might have several Custom Digests covering different things. Suggestions:

- "Strategic Accounts Weekly Review"
    
- "Daily Releases Recap"
    
- "Onboarding Cohort Q2 — Weekly Status"
    
- "Renewals Pipeline — Monthly"
    

### Scope[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#scope)

This is the most open-ended part of Custom Digest setup. You can configure:

- **Customer scope** — specific accounts, a segment, a customer lifecycle stage, or a saved customer list
    
- **Team scope** — specific teams, [Squads](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/squads-and-segments), or individuals
    
- **The combination** — e.g., "Enterprise CSMs working with Strategic accounts"
    

Scope is where Custom Digests get their flexibility. A well-scoped Custom Digest produces sharp, useful output. A poorly-scoped one produces generic noise. Spend time on this step.

### KPIs and metrics[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#kpis-and-metrics)

Pick the [KPIs](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/kpis) the digest should focus on. Unlike the Coaching Plan, Custom Digests don't have an "always visible" mechanic — the KPIs you select are what the digest analyzes.

Match the KPIs to the digest's purpose:

- A renewal pipeline digest might focus on Approaching Renewals, Engagement Trend, and Multi-Threading Health
    
- A daily releases recap might focus on Tickets Created, Tickets Completed, and Internal Activity
    
- A strategic accounts review might focus on Customer Meeting Time, Executive Engagement, and Total Touchpoints
    

### Schedule[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#schedule)

**Custom Digests support flexible scheduling**, unlike the standard types. Available frequencies typically include:

- **Weekly** — the most common; pick a day and time
    
- **Every weekday** — for daily monitoring (Monday through Friday)
    
- **Daily (every day)** — for high-frequency monitoring including weekends
    
- **Monthly** — for slower-cadence reviews
    

For each frequency you also configure day, time, time zone, and lookback period.

The schedule should match the digest's purpose:

- Weekly is right for most "review" use cases
    
- Every weekday makes sense for "what happened today" recaps tied to a daily work rhythm
    
- Monthly is right for "state of [thing]" reports that don't need finer granularity
    

### Recipients[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#recipients)

Same flexibility as other digest types. Add anyone who needs the digest's view — yourself, your team, leadership, cross-functional partners, or a Slack channel (see [Recipients & Schedule](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/recipients-and-schedule) for the Slack channel email pro tip).

### Send a test[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#send-a-test)

Always click **Send Test** before saving. Custom Digests are more open-ended than the other types, which means there's more room for the configuration to produce something different from what you expected. Verify the output reads the way you want before committing it to a recurring schedule.

## Examples of useful Custom Digests[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#examples-of-useful-custom-digests)

A few patterns that customers consistently find valuable:

### "Strategic Accounts Weekly Review"[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#strategic-accounts-weekly-review)

- **Scope:** A specific list of high-value or high-touch customer accounts
    
- **KPIs:** Customer Meeting Time, Engagement Trend, Total Touchpoints, Open Tickets
    
- **Schedule:** Weekly, Monday morning
    
- **Recipients:** Account team leads + CS leadership
    

Surfaces what's happening on the accounts that matter most without diluting attention with the broader book.

### "Onboarding Cohort Status"[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#onboarding-cohort-status)

- **Scope:** Customers in a specific lifecycle stage (e.g., "first 90 days")
    
- **KPIs:** Time-to-Value, Implementation Progress, Customer Participation, Tech Blocker Time
    
- **Schedule:** Weekly
    
- **Recipients:** Onboarding team + CS leadership
    

Tracks the cohort moving through onboarding without requiring a separate dashboard rebuild.

### "Daily Release Recap"[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#daily-release-recap)

- **Scope:** A specific team or [Squad](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/squads-and-segments) — typically engineering or product
    
- **KPIs:** Tickets Completed, Internal Activity, custom deployment metrics
    
- **Schedule:** Every weekday, end-of-day
    
- **Recipients:** Eng leadership + relevant cross-functional partners
    

A daily "what shipped today" summary that arrives without anyone running a manual report.

### "Cross-Functional Partner Monthly"[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#cross-functional-partner-monthly)

- **Scope:** Customer accounts or teams relevant to a non-CS function (e.g., Product)
    
- **KPIs:** A handful of relevant metrics matching that function's interests
    
- **Schedule:** Monthly, first Monday
    
- **Recipients:** The cross-functional partner team
    

A way to give a partner team visibility into your data without making them log in.

## Common pitfalls[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#common-pitfalls)

### "The output is too generic"[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#the-output-is-too-generic)

Custom Digests are vulnerable to vague configuration. The more specific the scope and KPIs, the sharper the output. If your digest reads like it could apply to any team or any time period, narrow the scope.

### "I have too many Custom Digests"[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#i-have-too-many-custom-digests)

If you find yourself with 8+ Custom Digests, consider whether you really need them all or whether a few of them could be consolidated. Digest fatigue is real — both for you and for any recipients you've added.

### "The schedule doesn't match the cadence I actually use it"[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#the-schedule-doesnt-match-the-cadence-i-actually-use-it)

Easy to misconfigure when you're setting up. If a weekly digest is being read every other week, drop it to bi-weekly (via two delivery configurations). If a daily digest is being skipped on weekends, change it from "Daily (every day)" to "Daily (weekdays)."

### "It doesn't fit one of my real use cases"[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest#it-doesnt-fit-one-of-my-real-use-cases)

If you're spending a lot of time wrestling a Custom Digest into shape and it still doesn't match what you need, that's a signal. Either the data Lumopath collects doesn't quite cover the use case (in which case the answer might be a different tool), or one of the standard digest types would actually serve you better than you think.
