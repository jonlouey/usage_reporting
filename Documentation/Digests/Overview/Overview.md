AI-generated summaries of your team's activity, customer health, or organization performance — delivered to your inbox on a schedule you choose.

Digests are scheduled, AI-generated reports that arrive in your inbox without you having to log in to Lumopath. They turn the dashboards and metrics you care about into a narrative summary you can read in two minutes — coaching insights for your team, performance summaries for leadership, or custom reports tailored to your business.

For most managers and leaders using Lumopath, digests become the most valuable feature in the product. They turn Lumopath from "a thing I log into" into "a thing that keeps me informed."
