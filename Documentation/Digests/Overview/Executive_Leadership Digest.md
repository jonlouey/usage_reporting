The "what's most important if I was out of office last week" digest. Org-level health, risks, and wins for senior leaders managing managers.

**The Executive / Leadership Digest answers a single question every week: if I had been out of office last week, what are the most important things for me to know?** It's the digest senior leaders use to make sure nothing critical slipped past them.

It is **not** a dashboard substitute. It is not a per-employee coaching brief. It is not a per-KPI rollup. It's a curated weekly summary of the things at the org or team level that actually warrant a leader's attention.

## Why this digest[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#why-this-digest)

Senior leaders — VPs, Directors, Heads of CS, CCOs — face a specific problem with operational data. The dashboards exist, the metrics are tracked, and yet the most important things that happened last week are the ones easiest to miss because they're buried in normalcy. A team that quietly drifted into burnout. An account segment that suddenly stopped engaging. A manager whose team is showing the early signs of strain. These get noticed eventually, but eventually is often too late.

The Executive Digest's job is to surface those things at the start of every week, in a format a leader can read in two minutes. The framing — "what should I know if I was OOO last week?" — is the litmus test. If something would have been worth telling a returning leader, it's in the digest. If not, it isn't.

## What it contains[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#what-it-contains)

Each delivery is structured around the most important signals at the org or team level:

- **Overall health** — Where the org is right now on the metrics that matter, with the trend vs. prior week
    
- **Notable risks** — Anything that deteriorated this week and warrants attention (a team's [capacity](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/capacity) crossing a threshold, a segment's engagement dropping, a manager's burnout count rising)
    
- **Notable wins** — Improvements, recoveries, accounts that turned around, teams that hit a milestone
    
- **Anything systemic** — Patterns that cross multiple teams or segments (process drag, reorg fallout, an initiative consuming more time than expected)
    
- **Specific callouts when warranted** — The digest doesn't drill into individual employees or per-KPI breakdowns by default, but it _will_ call out a specific person, team, or KPI when something material is happening that the leader needs to act on or be aware of
    

The output is a narrative summary, not a dashboard rendering. Most leaders find each delivery is 5–10 minutes of reading.

## Who it's for[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#who-its-for)

The Executive / Leadership Digest is built for **leaders who manage managers**. Specifically:

- VPs of Customer Success, Account Management, Support, or Revenue
    
- Directors and Heads of teams that include other people managers
    
- Cross-functional leaders who need a recurring read on a CS or AM org's health
    
- Executives who want a regular check on the people side of the business without sitting in another standing meeting
    

It is **not** designed for:

- Front-line managers running 1:1s — they should use the [Weekly Coaching Plan](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan) instead, which is per-employee and built around coaching prep
    
- ICs reviewing their own day — they should use the [Daily IC Briefing](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/daily-ic-briefing)

## Setup[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#setup)

The general digest creation flow is covered in [Setting Up a Digest](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/setting-up-a-digest). Choices specific to the Executive Digest:

### Digest Name[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#digest-name)

Pick a name you'll recognize on the listing. Suggestions:

- "[Your name]'s Weekly Exec Digest"
    
- "CS Org Weekly Review"
    
- "Weekly Leadership Digest — [Region or Department]"
    

### Scope[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#scope)

Pick the org or team scope the digest should cover:

- **Your full org** (all teams reporting up through you) — the most common pattern
    
- **A specific segment or** [**Squad**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/squads-and-segments) — useful if you want a digest narrowed to a specific business unit
    
- **Multiple teams individually** — possible but usually less useful than a single broader scope; the leader pattern is the rollup, not the comparison
    

The Executive Digest uses the full data behind whatever scope you pick. There is no per-KPI selection step like the Coaching Plan — the AI surfaces the relevant signals automatically based on what changed.

### Recipients[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#recipients)

Common patterns:

- **Just yourself** — for personal weekly review
    
- **You + your boss** — gives your manager the same shared picture
    
- **You + peer leaders** — for cross-functional alignment (e.g., the VP of Sales gets your CS leadership digest for context)
    
- **You + your direct reports (the managers)** — gives your management layer the same view you have
    
- **A leadership channel in Slack** — see [Recipients & Schedule](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/recipients-and-schedule) for the Slack channel email pro tip
    

Recipients see the same digest you see. Don't add someone if you'd be uncomfortable with them seeing the org-level signals as written.

### Schedule[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#schedule)

**The Executive Digest is hardcoded to weekly cadence — you can't change the frequency.** What you can change:

- **Day of the week** — most leaders pick Monday morning so it's part of the start-of-week ritual; some prefer Friday afternoon as a "what just happened?" wrap-up
    
- **Time of day** — typically 9:00 AM in the leader's local time zone
    
- **Time zone** — anchor for delivery timing
    

The default and recommended schedule:

- **Monday at 9:00 AM** in your local time zone
    

### Need more frequent updates, or different versions for different audiences?[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#need-more-frequent-updates-or-different-versions-for-different-audiences)

Because the Executive Digest is weekly-only, the way to handle more granular needs is to **create multiple digests**. Common patterns:

- **A Monday morning digest for yourself** plus **a Friday afternoon digest with the same scope sent to your VP** — both use the same data; the timing serves different audiences
    
- **A digest scoped to your CS org** plus **a separate digest scoped to a specific segment you're focused on this quarter** — same digest type, different scopes
    
- **A digest sent only to you for personal review** plus **a sanitized version sent to a broader leadership Slack channel** — manage which signals you want broader visibility on
    

There's no cap on the number of digests you can create.

### Send a test[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#send-a-test)

Before saving, click **Send Test** to email yourself a sample. Verify:

- The right org or team scope is selected
    
- The narrative covers the level of detail you actually want
    
- The recipients list includes everyone it should
    

If the digest reads more like a per-employee dashboard than a leadership summary, your scope is probably too narrow — broaden it to the org level.

## Using it well[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#using-it-well)

The Executive Digest is designed to be read fast and acted on selectively. The pattern that works:

### The 5-minute Monday read[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#the-5-minute-monday-read)

1. **Open it first thing Monday morning.** Don't wait until you have time to "really go through it." The whole point is that two minutes of skimming are enough.
    
2. **Scan top to bottom** without stopping to look for: the headline trend, anything flagged as a risk, anything labeled systemic.
    
3. **For each risk or callout**, decide whether you'll act this week or note it for later. Most weeks you'll have 1–2 things worth a Slack to a manager or an agenda item for an upcoming meeting.
    
4. **Skim the wins** — these are the things to recognize publicly or use in leadership communications.
    
5. **Move on.**
    

### What to do with the narrative[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#what-to-do-with-the-narrative)

The Executive Digest's narrative is intentionally written to be quotable. Two common uses:

- **Forward sections to your skip-level reports.** "The digest flagged that Enterprise is at 112% capacity this week — what's going on with the team there?" is a more useful Slack than asking generally.
    
- **Paste into leadership updates.** A weekly "state of CS" message to your peer execs becomes much easier when half of it is already written. Read the digest, edit lightly, send.
    

### What _not_ to do[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#what-not-to-do)

- **Don't try to act on every signal.** The Executive Digest is intentionally curated to ~5–10 minutes of reading. If you find yourself trying to follow up on everything, you're reading it as a task list instead of a status update. Pick the 1–2 things that matter most and let the next week's digest catch what's still relevant.
    
- **Don't use it as a substitute for talking to your managers.** The digest tells you _what_ is happening. Your conversations with managers tell you _why_. Both matter.
    
- **Don't share the full digest with people who aren't part of your leadership context.** The digest is candid about org-level risks. Forwarding it to the wider team can cause more anxiety than it solves.
    

## Common pitfalls[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#common-pitfalls)

### "It feels too high-level"[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#it-feels-too-high-level)

Possible causes:

- **Your scope is too broad** — a digest covering 200 people across 15 teams will inevitably be high-level. Narrow to a specific org or segment if you want sharper signal.
    
- **You should be using a Coaching Plan instead** — if you find yourself wanting per-employee detail, the Executive Digest isn't the right tool. Front-line management is what the [Weekly Coaching Plan](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan) is for.
    

### "It feels redundant with what I already know"[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#it-feels-redundant-with-what-i-already-know)

This is usually a positive signal — the digest is confirming your read. The value of the Executive Digest isn't in surprising you every week; it's in catching the weeks when something _isn't_ what you expected. Don't pause it because it's quiet for a stretch; the value compounds when something does come up.

### "It's not catching things I think it should"[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest#its-not-catching-things-i-think-it-should)

Two usual causes:

- **The relevant integrations aren't connected** — the Coach reasons over what Lumopath can see. If the signal lives in a tool that isn't integrated, it won't appear. Check **Settings → Integrations**.
    
- **The scope is filtering it out** — verify the scope includes the team or segment where the signal is actually showing up.
    

For more on data quality issues, see [Data Quality Gotchas](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/data-quality-gotchas).
