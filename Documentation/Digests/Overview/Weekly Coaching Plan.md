Per-employee AI coaching analysis delivered weekly. The most-used digest type for managers running 1:1s.

**The Weekly Coaching Plan is per-employee AI coaching analysis delivered to your inbox on a schedule you choose — typically Monday morning.** For each person on your team, the digest produces a section covering what changed this week, how they compare to peers, what to recognize, what to coach on, and a suggested next step for your 1:1.

It's the most-used digest type, and for most managers it's the fastest way to get value from Lumopath. Setting one up takes about two minutes.

## Why this digest[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#why-this-digest)

If you manage a team, the hardest part of running good 1:1s isn't the conversation — it's the prep. You walk into the room and you have ten people on your roster, each with their own context, and you have ten minutes to know what to talk about. The Weekly Coaching Plan does that prep for you.

By Monday morning, you have a per-person brief that surfaces:

- Whose accounts are showing signs of getting ahead or falling behind
    
- Who's spending time on the wrong work — internal overhead, the wrong customers, or low-leverage tasks that crowd out the work that matters
    
- Whose [capacity](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/capacity) is trending wrong and why
    
- Who quietly performed well and deserves recognition
    
- One specific, actionable thing to discuss in each person's 1:1
    

You don't have to log in. You don't have to scan a dashboard. You read the email, pick what matters, walk into the meeting.

## What it contains[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#what-it-contains)

Each delivery contains a section per team member. Inside each person's section, you can expect:

- **A short summary of what changed this week** — capacity movement, account activity, notable wins or escalations
    
- **Per-employee AI analysis** drawing on capacity, [KPIs](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/kpis) you selected, and peer comparisons
    
- **Recognition opportunities** — what they did well that's worth calling out in the 1:1
    
- **Coaching observations** — patterns the manager should investigate or address
    
- **A suggested next step** — one concrete thing to bring into the conversation
    

The analysis is currently delivered as text. You read each section like a brief — it's not a dashboard. Most managers find it takes 5–10 minutes to read through a digest covering 8–12 people.

## Who it's for[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#who-its-for)

The Weekly Coaching Plan is built for **front-line managers running regular 1:1s**. Specifically:

- CS Managers, Support Managers, Account Management leads
    
- Anyone with 5–15 direct reports they meet with weekly or biweekly
    
- People who want their 1:1 prep to come to them, not require an extra workflow
    

It is **not** designed for:

- Senior leaders who manage other managers — they should use the [Executive / Leadership Digest](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest) instead
    
- ICs reviewing their own performance — IC-facing digests are in active design and not yet shipping
    

## Setup[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#setup)

The general digest creation flow is covered in [Setting Up a Digest](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/setting-up-a-digest). Here are the choices specific to Weekly Coaching Plan:

### Digest Name[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#digest-name)

Pick a name you'll recognize on the listing. Suggestions:

- "[Your name]'s Coaching Plan"
    
- "[Team name] Coaching Plan"
    
- "Weekly Coaching — [Squad name]"
    

### Team Members to Coach[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#team-members-to-coach)

Pick the people you want covered. **The cap is 10 team members per digest.** If you manage more than 10 people, create a second Coaching Plan for the additional members — see "Need to coach more than 10 people?" below.

You can mix individuals and pull from teams or [Squads](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/squads-and-segments).

### Metrics to Analyze[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#metrics-to-analyze)

Pick the [KPIs](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/kpis) you want the digest to consider for each person. **The cap is 10 KPIs per digest, with up to 3 marked as "always visible."** Understanding the difference matters:

- **Always-visible KPIs** appear in every weekly delivery for every person, whether or not anything interesting happened on that metric. These are your foundational metrics — the things you want to see at a glance every Monday regardless.
    
- **Other KPIs** (the remaining 7) are conditionally surfaced — they appear in a person's section only when something noteworthy is happening (a notable change, an outlier, an anomaly). This keeps the digest focused on signal instead of churn.
    

Pick your 3 always-visible KPIs carefully. They're the ones you want to track religiously for every person, every week. Common picks:

- **Customer Meeting Time** — if customer-facing time is what matters most for the role
    
- **Engagement Trend** — for early churn signals
    
- **Accounts Lacking Recent Contact** — for catching at-risk accounts
    
- **Customer Email Response Time** — if responsiveness is the core metric
    
- **Customer Emails Sent** — for tracking outreach activity
    
- **Capacity** _(implicitly tracked alongside the others; useful as context for whatever else you pick)_
    

The other 7 (conditional) KPIs give the AI Coach more dimensions to flag interesting patterns without cluttering every delivery. Pick KPIs you care about but don't need to see weekly unless something changes.

The KPIs you pick shape the per-person sections. Three sharp always-visible picks plus a handful of conditional ones produces better coaching prompts than ten generic always-visible KPIs.

### Recipients[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#recipients)

Just yourself, plus optionally:

- **Your manager** — gives them visibility into your team's coaching cadence without an extra meeting
    
- **A peer manager** — useful for cross-team alignment
    
- **Your full team** — uncommon for coaching digests because the analysis is candid; verify with people first
    

Recipients see the full digest. Don't add someone if you'd be uncomfortable with them seeing the per-person sections.

For more on recipients (including the Slack channel pro tip), see [Recipients & Schedule](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/recipients-and-schedule).

### Schedule[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#schedule)

**The Coaching Plan is hardcoded to weekly cadence — you can't change the frequency.** What you can change:

- **Day of the week** — most managers pick Monday so the digest is ready for the week's 1:1s
    
- **Time of day** — typically 9:00 AM, but Sunday evening is also common
    
- **Time zone** — anchor for delivery timing
    

The default and recommended schedule:

- **Monday at 9:00 AM** in your local time zone
    

This lines up with the start of the work week so you have prep before Monday meetings start.

### Need to coach more than 10 people, or want different timing per person?[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#need-to-coach-more-than-10-people-or-want-different-timing-per-person)

Because of the 10-person cap and the weekly-only frequency, the way to handle larger teams or more granular timing is **to create multiple Coaching Plans**. Common patterns:

- **One Coaching Plan per person**, each scheduled to arrive the morning before that person's 1:1. If you have 5 reports running 1:1s on different days, that's 5 separate Coaching Plans, one per name, each timed for their specific day.
    
- **Two or three Coaching Plans split by sub-team** — e.g., "Enterprise CSMs Coaching Plan" and "Mid-Market CSMs Coaching Plan" — each with up to 10 people and tuned to that group's KPIs.
    
- **A broad team-wide Coaching Plan** for general awareness, plus **a focused 3-person Coaching Plan** for the people you're actively coaching this period.
    

There's no cap on the number of digests you can create. The constraint is your willingness to manage them.

### Send a test[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#send-a-test)

Before saving, click **Send Test** to email yourself a sample. Verify:

- The right people are included
    
- The KPIs you picked make sense in the per-person sections
    
- The narrative output reads the way you expected
    

If something looks off, adjust the configuration before saving. It's much easier to fix now than after the digest has been sending the wrong thing.

## Using it well[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#using-it-well)

Once the Coaching Plan is live, the goal is to make reading it part of your Monday morning routine.

### The 5-minute pattern[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#the-5-minute-pattern)

Most managers settle on this rhythm:

1. **Open the digest first thing Monday morning** (or Sunday evening)
    
2. **Scan top to bottom** without stopping to look for anything that surprises you
    
3. **For each person you have a 1:1 with this week**, read their section and pick the 1–2 things you'll bring up
    
4. **Add any account-level concerns to your action list** for the day
    
5. **Move on**
    

That's it. You don't need to memorize anything. The digest is your prep notes — you can re-open it during the 1:1 itself.

### What to do with the "Next Step" line[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#what-to-do-with-the-next-step-line)

Each person's section typically includes a suggested next step. Treat it as a draft, not a script. The AI Coach is good at synthesizing the data, but you have the context — sometimes the suggestion is exactly right, sometimes it needs adjusting, sometimes there's something more important you noticed that the digest didn't catch.

The point of the next-step line is to give you a specific starting point so you don't walk into the 1:1 wondering what to ask. Use it as a scaffold.

### What _not_ to do[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#what-not-to-do)

- **Don't forward the digest to the person it's about.** The analysis is candid coaching prep written for the manager. It's not a performance review and it's not designed for the employee to read directly.
    
- **Don't try to act on every signal in every digest.** Pick the 1–2 highest-leverage things per person and ignore the rest. The next digest will surface what's still relevant.
    
- **Don't treat the digest as the only data point.** Pair it with what you know about the person and their week. The digest is a starting point, not a verdict.
    

## Common pitfalls[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#common-pitfalls)

### "I picked too many always-visible KPIs and the digest is overwhelming"[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#i-picked-too-many-always-visible-kpis-and-the-digest-is-overwhelming)

This is the most common first-week issue. Remember: you can have up to 10 KPIs total, but only 3 should be marked "always visible." If all 10 of your KPIs are always-visible, every section will include all 10 metrics for every person, every week — that's the overwhelm. Edit the digest, drop the always-visible count to your 3 highest-priority metrics, and let the other 7 surface conditionally when something interesting happens.

### "Some people are missing from the digest"[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#some-people-are-missing-from-the-digest)

Either they're not in the **Team Members to Coach** list, or they're in the list but you've hit the per-digest cap. Edit the digest and verify the list. If you have more than 10 people, create a second digest for the additional members.

### "The analysis is generic"[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan#the-analysis-is-generic)

Two usual causes:

- Too many or too generic KPIs (try cutting to 3 specific ones)
    
- Insufficient connected integrations (the Coach can only reason over data Lumopath sees — check [Setup → Integrations](https://learn.lumopath.ai/setup-and-administration/integrations))
    

For more on data quality issues, see [Data Quality Gotchas](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/data-quality-gotchas).
