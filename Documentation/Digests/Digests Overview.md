What digests are, why they matter, and the types available today.

**A digest is a scheduled, AI-generated report that arrives in your inbox.** You set it up once — pick a type, choose what it covers, set the schedule, choose who gets it — and from then on it shows up in your inbox automatically. The AI Coach generates the analysis fresh each time, drawing on the latest activity data Lumopath has collected.

Digests are how Lumopath becomes a thing that keeps you informed instead of a thing you log into.

## Why digests matter[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/overview#why-digests-matter)

Most managers and leaders don't open a dashboard every day. They have a meeting, a Slack thread, a fire to put out. The risk in any analytics product is that the value lives behind a login that gets visited once a week, on a good week. Digests solve that by inverting the model: the analysis comes to you.

A few specific things digests do well:

- **Make sure nothing important gets missed.** A weekly coaching digest tells you which of your reports has had a capacity spike, which accounts are silently going cold, and which person you should be recognizing this week — even if you wouldn't have thought to look.
    
- **Save the time of generating reports manually.** Instead of opening Team Overview, scanning the metrics, copy-pasting into a leadership update, and writing the narrative — you can have the same output emailed to you on the schedule you choose.
    
- **Give leadership shared context without you doing the work.** Add your VP as a recipient on an Executive Digest and you've replaced a recurring "what's the team status?" Slack with a self-serve answer.

### Weekly Coaching Plan[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/overview#weekly-coaching-plan)

**Per-employee AI coaching analysis for managers running 1:1s.** For each person on the team you select, the digest produces a section covering what changed this week, how they compare to peers, what to recognize, what to coach on, and a suggested next step for the conversation.

The most-used digest type. Hardcoded to weekly cadence (you choose the day and time) and typically scheduled for Monday morning so managers walk into their week already knowing where to focus.

See the full [Weekly Coaching Plan page](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/weekly-coaching-plan).

### Executive / Leadership Digest[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/overview#executive-leadership-digest)

**"What's most important if I was out of office last week?"** Aggregates the org-level signals that a senior leader needs to be aware of: overall team health, emerging risks, notable wins, anything that crosses a threshold worth a leader's attention. Doesn't drill into individual employees or per-KPI breakdowns unless something specific warrants a callout — the goal is to make sure nothing critical is missed, not to substitute for the dashboard.

Hardcoded to weekly cadence; typically sent to VPs, Directors, and Heads of CS, though some leaders also forward it to peer leaders or finance.

See the full [Executive / Leadership Digest page](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/executive-leadership-digest).

### Daily IC Briefing[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/overview#daily-ic-briefing)

**Daily action priorities for ICs managing customer accounts.** Each morning, the digest ranks the IC's accounts by urgency and surfaces what's going on with each: contact gaps, escalations, engagement drops, expansion signals. The IC opens it before their day starts and walks in already knowing which 3–5 accounts need attention today.

The IC equivalent of the Coaching Plan for Managers and the Executive Digest for Leaders. The fastest way for a CSM or AM to start their day with data instead of inbox.

See the full [Daily IC Briefing page](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/daily-ic-briefing).

### Custom Digest[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/overview#custom-digest)

**Configurable digest for use cases not covered by the standard types.** Lets you build a digest around the specific [KPIs](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/kpis), teams, segments, or customer scopes that matter to your business. Useful for things like "weekly review of strategic accounts" or "every weekday release recap" — anything that doesn't fit the other types.

Custom Digests support more flexible scheduling than the role-specific types — weekly, every weekday, monthly, etc. — depending on the use case.

See the full [Custom Digest page](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests/custom-digest).

## How digests work in Lumopath[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/overview#how-digests-work-in-lumopath)

A digest has four ingredients you configure when you create it:

1. **Type** — Weekly Coaching Plan, Executive/Leadership, or Custom.
    
2. **Data scope** — Which teams, individuals, [Squads](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/squads-and-segments), or customer scope the digest covers. The Coaching Plan also asks which [KPIs](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/kpis) to analyze.
    
3. **Recipients** — Who receives the email. Typically yourself, optionally your boss, your team, or anyone else who needs the same view.
    
4. **Schedule** — When it goes out (frequency, day, time, time zone) and what time range each delivery analyzes.
    

Once it's set up, it runs on its own. You can pause it, edit it, send a test, or delete it from the [Digests page](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/overview) at any time — see [Pausing, Editing, Deleting](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/pausing-editing-deleting).

## Where to find digests in the app[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/overview#where-to-find-digests-in-the-app)

Digests live under **Digests** in the left navigation. The page shows every digest you've created, with each row displaying the digest's name, type, the team or scope it covers, the recipients, and the schedule.
