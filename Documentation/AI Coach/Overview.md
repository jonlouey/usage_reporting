The AI-powered analyst built into Lumopath. Reasons over your team's actual activity data to answer specific questions about coaching, capacity, and customer health.

The AI Coach is built into every dashboard in Lumopath. It's the fastest way to get from "I see something interesting in this dashboard" to "I know what to do about it." Most customers find the Coach quickly becomes the part of the product they reach for daily.

This section is the complete guide to the AI Coach.

## In this section[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/ai-coach#in-this-section)

- [**Overview**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/overview) — What the AI Coach is, what makes it different from a general-purpose chatbot, and when to reach for it.
    
- [**How to Use the AI Coach**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/how-to-use) — Hands-on procedural guide: where the button lives, how filters work, how to ask follow-up questions.
    
- [**Prompt Library**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/prompt-library) — Thirty-three curated prompts organized by what you're trying to do — from "prep my next 1:1" to "prep for this customer's renewal." Copy, paste, adjust the variables, and run.
    
- [**Effective Prompting**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/effective-prompting) — Principles for writing your own prompts. The difference between a question that gets a useful answer and one that doesn't.
    
- [**Where It Appears**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/where-it-appears) — The list of dashboards and views where the Summarize button lives today.
    
- [**What It Can and Can't Do**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/what-it-can-and-cant-do) — Honest expectation-setting. What the Coach is good at, what it isn't, and the things people sometimes expect it to do that it can't.
    

## Where to start[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/ai-coach#where-to-start)

If you're new to the AI Coach: read [Overview](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/overview) and [How to Use](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/how-to-use), then open the [Prompt Library](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/prompt-library) and try one of the prompts on your team's data.

If you want to understand what's actually happening under the hood — what the Coach can see, what it can't, and how filters define its context window — see [How the AI Coach Reasons](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/how-the-ai-coach-reasons) in the foundation section.
