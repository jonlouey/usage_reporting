
The list of dashboards and views where the Summarize button lives today.

The AI Coach is available across many views in Lumopath. Look for the **Summarize** or **AI Coach** button (the label varies by page) — that's where the Coach lives.

## Available today[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/where-it-appears#available-today)

View

What the Coach is useful for

[**Team Overview**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/feature-reference/team-overview)

Team-level capacity, coaching summaries, identifying outliers, building leadership updates.

[**Customer Details**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/feature-reference/customer-details)

Single-account analysis — account health, engagement trends, churn risk signals, meeting prep.

[**Customer Prioritization**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/feature-reference/customer-prioritization)

Account-level questions across your book — which accounts need attention, which are at risk, how to prioritize your day.

[**Customer Prioritization insight drawer**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/feature-reference/customer-prioritization#ai-coach-in-the-drawer)

Quick follow-up questions about a specific account without leaving the table view. A compact mini Coach inside the drawer.

[**Cost to Serve**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/feature-reference/cost-to-serve)

Identifying time sinks vs. neglected accounts, asking specific questions about the scatter plot.

**Segment Details**

Comparing performance and effort patterns across customer segments.

**Individual employee detail pages**

Per-person analysis: capacity drivers, time allocation, coaching recommendations.

## How to invoke it[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/where-it-appears#how-to-invoke-it)

In every view above, the workflow is the same:

1. **Set your filters** at the top of the dashboard (team, time range, customer, etc.).
    
2. **Click the Summarize or AI Coach button** — usually in the top-right area of the page or a section header.
    
3. **Pick a prompt or type your own question.**
    

The **Customer Prioritization insight drawer** is an exception: the mini Coach appears inline inside the drawer when you click an account row. Type your question directly in the drawer's chat input — no separate button click needed.

For step-by-step instructions, see [How to Use the AI Coach](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/how-to-use).

## Coverage notes[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/where-it-appears#coverage-notes)

The Summarize button isn't on every screen in Lumopath today — only the ones above. If you're in a view that doesn't have it, the most common workaround is to navigate to a related view that does. For example, if you want AI analysis of a team's behavior across a specific customer segment, set the filters on Team Overview and use the Coach there.

If there's a screen where you'd find the Coach especially valuable that doesn't currently have it, that's worth flagging to [support@lumopath.ai](mailto:support@lumopath.ai) — coverage continues to expand based on customer feedback.
