What the AI Coach is, what makes it different from a general chatbot, and when to reach for it.

**The AI Coach is a built-in analyst that reasons over your team's actual activity data to answer specific questions about coaching, capacity, and customer health.** You ask it a question, it pulls from the data Lumopath has already collected, and it gives you back a structured, contextualized answer in seconds.

## What makes it different[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/overview#what-makes-it-different)

Plenty of tools have AI assistants built in. The AI Coach is different because of what it can see.

A general-purpose LLM like ChatGPT or Claude can reason about coaching frameworks in the abstract — what makes a good 1:1, how to spot burnout, when to rebalance accounts. It can give you good general advice. What it can't do is answer the question grounded in _your_ team's specific situation this week.

The AI Coach can. It has access to:

- The actual activity data Lumopath collects from your connected tools
    
- Your team's reporting structure, account ownership, and customer relationships
    
- The metrics Lumopath builds — [Capacity](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/capacity), [KPIs](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/kpis), [Reactive vs Proactive time](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/reactive-vs-proactive), engagement trends, and more
    

That combination — broad reasoning capability plus your specific data — is the point. It's the difference between "here's how to think about churn risk" and "here's why Acme is at risk — Jamie Torres went silent after the March QBR, your team's contact frequency has dropped from weekly to once in three weeks, and Pendo shows their primary champion logged in once last month after a daily average of 14 sessions." That kind of specificity is what lets the Coach turn a question into a decision.

## When to reach for it[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/overview#when-to-reach-for-it)

The Coach is most useful when you're trying to do one of these:

- **Understand** _**why**_ **a number is what it is.** "Why is this person's capacity high" is much more useful than knowing their capacity is high.
    
- **Get a narrative summary you can use elsewhere.** Headcount conversations, leadership updates, 1:1 prep, board slides — the Coach generates structured analysis you can quote or adapt.
    
- **Spot patterns you'd miss scanning the dashboard.** "Compare engagement between top performers and bottom performers" or "what changed between the first half of this period and the second" surface things that aren't obvious from individual cards.
    
- **Get a starting point when you don't know what to ask.** Click Summarize without typing anything and the Coach generates a baseline overview of whatever you're looking at.
    

## What it isn't[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/overview#what-it-isnt)

A few things to set expectations:

- **It's not a search engine for your inbox.** The Coach reasons over metadata (who, when, how often, response time) and AI-classified topics. It doesn't quote individual emails, messages, or call transcripts.
    
- **It's not a replacement for the dashboard.** The Coach is a fast layer on top of the data — sometimes the right move is to read its summary, then click into the underlying view to see specifics.
    
- **It's not a fortune teller.** It identifies patterns and risks based on what's happened, not predictions about what will happen. Its risk signals are flags worth investigating, not forecasts.
    

For the full conceptual picture of what the Coach can and can't see, and why filters define its answers, see [How the AI Coach Reasons](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/how-the-ai-coach-reasons).

## Anatomy of an interaction[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/overview#anatomy-of-an-interaction)

Every AI Coach session follows the same shape:

1. **Set your filters** on the dashboard you're looking at — team, time range, customer, segment.
    
2. **Click the Summarize button** to open the Coach.
    
3. **Pick a prompt or type your own question.** The [Prompt Library](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/prompt-library) has 33 curated starting points organized by job.
    
4. **Read the response, then iterate.** Ask follow-up questions, narrow the scope, or rephrase.
    
5. **Give feedback** with the thumbs-up / thumbs-down buttons. This trains the Coach for your organization specifically.
    

For the hands-on guide, see [How to Use the AI Coach](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/how-to-use).

## Privacy[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/overview#privacy)

A note about how Lumopath handles your data with the AI Coach:

- **Your data is not used to train models for other customers.** The Coach is constantly improving — but only for your organization, based on the feedback you provide.
    
- **The Coach respects your visibility settings.** If you don't have access to a person or account, the Coach doesn't either.
    
- **Dashboard filters bound the conversation.** The Coach can only reason over the data your current filters expose.
    

For the full story on data handling, see [Data & Access Management](https://learn.lumopath.ai/trust-and-privacy/data-and-access).
