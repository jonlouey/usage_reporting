
How to create, save, and manage your own custom AI Coach prompts. Build prompts tailored to your team's specific questions and workflows.

The [Prompt Library](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/prompt-library) gives you 33 curated prompts to start with, but you can also create and save your own. Custom prompts let you build questions tailored to your team's specific goals, metrics, and recurring needs — and share them with anyone at your organization.

## Where to find it[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/saved-prompts#where-to-find-it)

Go to **Settings → AI Coach Prompts** to view, create, and manage your saved prompts.

## Creating a new prompt[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/saved-prompts#creating-a-new-prompt)

Click **Create New Prompt** and fill in:

- **Prompt name** — A short title that describes what the prompt does (e.g., "Weekly team health check" or "Pre-QBR account summary")
    
- **Prompt text** — The full question or instruction you want the AI Coach to answer. Write it as if you were typing it directly into the Coach panel.
    
- **Variables** _(optional)_ — If your prompt needs to be customized each time (e.g., a person's name, a specific KPI, a date), include variables using `{{double curly braces}}`. When someone runs the prompt, the app will automatically ask them to fill in each variable before submitting.
    

**Tip:** Start by running a prompt manually in the AI Coach a few times, adjusting the wording until you get the output you want. Once it's working well, save it so you don't have to re-type it.

## What makes a good saved prompt[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/saved-prompts#what-makes-a-good-saved-prompt)

The best saved prompts are the ones you'd otherwise type from scratch every Monday morning. A few patterns:

- **Recurring questions** — "Summarize my team's performance this week against these specific KPIs" is worth saving if you ask it every week.
    
- **Role-specific prompts** — A prompt tailored to your team's specific role or segment ("Compare engagement patterns for our Enterprise accounts vs. Mid-Market this quarter") is more useful than a generic one.
    
- **Templates for common conversations** — A prompt that generates 1:1 prep, QBR prep, or leadership updates in the format you actually use saves time every cycle.
    

For inspiration, browse the [Prompt Library](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/prompt-library) — the 33 curated prompts there follow these same principles and can be adapted as starting points for your own.

## Managing saved prompts[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/saved-prompts#managing-saved-prompts)

From Settings → AI Coach Prompts, you can:

- **Edit** any prompt you've created — update the text, rename, or adjust variables
    
- **Delete** prompts you no longer need
    
- **View prompts created by others** in your organization
    

Saved prompts are available to everyone in your organization from the AI Coach panel on any dashboard.
