From login to first insight in under 5 minutes. A fast tour of the parts of Lumopath you'll use most — dashboards, the AI Coach, and your first digest.

This page gets you from "I just got a Lumopath login" to "I've seen something useful" in about 5 minutes. It's not the full guide to anything — it's the fast path to value so you can decide where to go deeper.

## Log in and orient[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/quickstart#log-in-and-orient)

Log in at [app.lumopath.ai](https://app.lumopath.ai/). Depending on your role, you'll land on one of two pages:

- **Managers and Leaders** land on [**Team Overview**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/feature-reference/team-overview) — the main dashboard showing your team's capacity, KPIs, time allocation, and customer engagement.
    
- **ICs** land on [**Customer Prioritization**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/feature-reference/customer-prioritization) — your accounts sorted by urgency, with the ones that need your attention at the top.
    

Take 30 seconds to read what's on the page. The numbers are real — they're drawn from the data your tools have been generating for the past 12 months.

## Try the AI Coach[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/quickstart#try-the-ai-coach)

The AI Coach is built into the dashboard you just landed on. Look for the **Summarize** button and click it.

A panel opens. You can either:

- **Pick one of the suggested prompts** (pre-loaded based on the page you're on) — just click and run
    
- **Type your own question** in plain language — try something like _"Summarize what's happening with my team this week"_ or _"Which accounts need the most attention?"_
    

The Coach's response is based on your actual data, not a generic template. Read it, then try a follow-up question — the Coach keeps context across the conversation.

**First-time tip:** The quality of AI Coach answers depends on what's connected. If the response feels thin, check **Settings → Integrations** to see which tools are linked. More integrations = more complete picture.

For the full guide to the AI Coach, including the curated prompt library with 33 ready-to-use prompts, see [**AI Coach**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/ai-coach).

## Set up your first digest[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/quickstart#set-up-your-first-digest)

A [Digest](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests) is a recurring AI-generated email that arrives in your inbox on a schedule — coaching prep, leadership updates, or account priorities, depending on the type. Most users find this is where Lumopath becomes a daily habit rather than a tool you occasionally log into.

Pick the digest that matches your role:

Go to **Digests** in the left navigation, click **Create Digest**, pick the type, configure it, and click **Send Test** to email yourself a preview. If it looks good, save it — the digest will start arriving on the schedule you set.

For the full setup guide, see [**Setting Up a Digest**](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/setting-up-a-digest).

## What's next[](https://learn.lumopath.ai/quickstart#whats-next)

You've logged in, tried the AI Coach, and set up your first digest. The next step depends on what you're trying to do:

- **Run a specific job** (capacity review, coaching prep, cost-to-serve analysis, account assignment) → Pick a [Workflow](https://learn.lumopath.ai/workflows/workflows) that matches your question.
    
- **Understand how the numbers are built** (what Capacity actually means, how KPIs work, what Reactive Time is) → Read through [How Lumopath Works](https://learn.lumopath.ai/how-lumopath-works/how-lumopath-works).
    
- **Get the most out of the AI Coach** (better prompts, understanding what it can and can't do) → Explore the [AI Coach zone](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/ai-coach), especially the [Prompt Library](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/prompt-library) and [Effective Prompting](https://learn.lumopath.ai/ai-coach/effective-prompting).
    
- **Set up more digests or adjust your existing one** → Browse the [Digests zone](https://learn.lumopath.ai/digests/digests).
    
- **Understand what Lumopath does and doesn't do with your data** → Read [What Lumopath Is (and Isn't)](https://learn.lumopath.ai/trust-and-privacy/what-lumopath-is-and-isnt).
    

If you're not sure, go back to the [Welcome page](https://learn.lumopath.ai/) and pick your role — each role's tab has a tailored starting path.
